## Q1. What is the difference between `<ol>` and `<ul>`? Give one example of when you would use each.

Your answer: An ordered list uses numbers for sequential items, like step-by-step game instructions. An unordered list uses bullet points for items where order does not matter, like a list of your favorite games.

## Q2. Why must every `<img>` have an `alt` attribute? Who uses it?

Your answer: The alt attribute gives a text description if your image fails to load on the screen. It is heavily used by visually impaired people who look at websites using talking screen readers.

## Q3. Your page links to `hobby.html` and to `https://...`. What is the difference between a relative link and an absolute link?

Your answer: A relative link connects files inside your own project folder, i.e. linking to your local hobby.html file. While an absolute link connects to a completely different website on the internet using a full URL like https://github.com.

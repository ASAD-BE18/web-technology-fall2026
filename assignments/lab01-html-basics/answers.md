## Q3. Your page links to `hobby.html` and to `https://...`. What is the difference between a relative link and an absolute link?

Your answer: A relative link connects files inside your own project folder, i.e. linking to your local hobby.html file. 
While an absolute link connects to a completely different website on the internet using a full URL like https://github.com.

## Q1. What is the difference between `<ol>` and `<ul>`? Give one example of when you would use each.

Your answer: '<ol>' is used to create a numbered list, while '<ul>' is used to create a bulleted list. I would use '<ol>' for cooking steps and '<ul>' for a list of my hobbies.

## Q2. Why must every `<img>` have an `alt` attribute? Who uses it?

Your answer: The alt attribute describes the image if it cannot be displayed. Screen readers use it to help visually impaired people understand what the image shows.

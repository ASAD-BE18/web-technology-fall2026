## Q1. What is the difference between `<ol>` and `<ul>`? Give one example of when you would use each.

Your answer:<ol> displays items in a numbered or ordered list.
<ul> displays items with bullet points. Use it when the order of items does not matter.

## Q2. Why must every `<img>` have an `alt` attribute? Who uses it?

Your answer:
<img> must have an alt attribute because it provides alternative text that describes the image.
## Q3. Your page links to `hobby.html` and to `https://...`. What is the difference between a relative link and an absolute link?

Your answer:
A relative link points to a page within the same website or project, while an absolute link contains the complete URL of a website.

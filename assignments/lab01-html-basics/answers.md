## Q1. What is the difference between `<ol>` and `<ul>`? Give one example of when you would use each.

Your answer:
The <ol> tag creates a numbered list when the order of items matters. The <ul> tag creates a bulleted list when the order does not matter. I would use <ol> for steps in recipe and <ul> to list the subjects I study at university.


## Q2. Why must every `<img>` have an `alt` attribute? Who uses it?

Your answer:
Alt attribute describes an image in case it cannot load or someone cannot see it. Screen readers use this text to help visually impaired people understand what the image shows.

## Q3. Your page links to `hobby.html` and to `https://...`. What is the difference between a relative link and an absolute link?

Your answer:
A relative link points to another file or page using its location in relation to the current page such as hobby.html.
Absolute link includes a complete web address such as https://web.whatsapp.com and can point to a page on another website.

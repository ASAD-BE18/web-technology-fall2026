## Q1. What is the difference between `<ol>` and `<ul>`? Give one example of when you would use each.

Your answer:
An ordered list creates a list in a specific order, like 1, 2, 3. On the other hand, an unordered list creates a bulleted list without a specific order. I would use an ordered list for my semester goals and an unordered list for my hobbies because hobbies do not need to be in a specific order.

## Q2. Why must every `<img>` have an `alt` attribute? Who uses it?

Your answer:
The alt attribute gives a text explanation of an image. It helps people who use screen readers to know what the image is about. It is also helpful when the image does not load.

## Q3. Your page links to `hobby.html` and to `https://...`. What is the difference between a relative link and an absolute link?

Your answer:
A relative link uses a file path like "hobby.html" to connect to another page inside my project. It depends on the location of the file. An absolute link includes the complete web address, such as "https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML", to open a page on a website. It works from different pages because it contains the complete URL.

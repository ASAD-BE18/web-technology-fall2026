## Q1. What is the difference between `<ol>` and `<ul>`? Give one example of when you would use each.

Your answer:<ol> creates a numbered list, while <ul> creates a bulleted list. I would use <ol> for steps in a recipe and <ul> for a list of hobbies.

## Q2. Why must every `<img>` have an `alt` attribute? Who uses it?

Your answer:The alt attribute describes an image if it cannot load. Screen readers also read this description for visually impaired users.

## Q3. Your page links to `hobby.html` and to `https://...`. What is the difference between a relative link and an absolute link?

Your answer:A relative link points to a file within the same website or project, such as hobby.html. An absolute link contains the full URL of a resource, such as https://www.example.com.

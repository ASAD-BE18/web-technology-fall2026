## Q1. In your form, why must a `<label>`'s `for` match its `<input>`'s `id`? What goes wrong if they don't match?

Your answer: Matching them links the text label to its input typing box so you can click the text to open the box. If they do not match the form becomes harder to use and screen reading tools for blind users will get confused.

## Q2. You used both `<section>` and `<article>`. What is the difference? Why did the hackathon story become an `<article>`?

Your answer: A section is just a part used to group similar things together on a page. An article is for a standalone piece of text that makes perfect sense by itself which is why a separate news story uses it.

## Q3. What is the difference between `<th>` and `<td>`? Why should a table never be used to lay out a whole page?

Your answer: A th is a bold headline cell at the top of a table, while a td is a normal text cell for data. You should never use tables to build a whole webpage layout because it breaks easily on small mobile phones.

## Q1. In your form, why must a `<label>`'s `for` match its `<input>`'s `id`? What goes wrong if they don't match?
Your answer:
The for attribute of a label must be the same as the id of the input field. This links the label to the input and makes it easier for users to click the label to select the field. If they are not the same the label might not function properly with the input.



## Q2. You used both `<section>` and `<article>`. What is the difference? Why did the hackathon story become an `<article>`?

Your answer:

I think a section groups related content on a webpage, for example, a timetable or a registration form. An article holds content that can make sense on its own. The hackathon story uses an article because it describes an event.

## Q3. What is the difference between `<th>` and `<td>`? Why should a table never be used to lay out a whole page?

Your answer:
The `<th>` tag is used for table headings for example Day, Activity and Time.
The `<td>` tag is used for data for example Monday and HTML Practice.
I believe that tables should display related data instead of arranging the whole webpage because semantic HTML elements are better for page structure.

## Q1. In your form, why must a `<label>`'s `for` match its `<input>`'s `id`? What goes wrong if they don't match?

The label's for attribute must match the input's id so the label connects to the correct input field. If they do not match, clicking the label may not focus the correct input, which can make the form harder to use.

## Q2. You used both `<section>` and `<article>`. What is the difference? Why did the hackathon story become an `<article>`?

A section> groups related content together, such as the timetable and the join form on my website. An <article> contains independent content that can make sense on its own, so I used it for the hackathon story.

## Q3. What is the difference between `<th>` and `<td>`? Why should a table never be used to lay out a whole page?

The <th> tag is used for table headings, while the <td> tag is used for ordinary data cells. Tables should display information in rows and columns, not arrange an entire webpage, because semantic HTML and CSS are better for page layout.

## Q1. In your form, why must a `<label>`'s `for` match its `<input>`'s `id`? What goes wrong if they don't match?

Your answer:  The for attribute of a label must match the id of its input to connect them correctly. If they do not match, clicking the label will not focus the correct input field, and the form may be harder to use.
## Q2. You used both `<section>` and `<article>`. What is the difference? Why did the hackathon story become an `<article>`?

Your answer:  A '<section>' groups related content about the same topic, while an '<article>' contains independent content that can be understood on its own. The hackathon story uses an '<article>' because it is a complete story that readers can understand separately from the rest of the webpage.

## Q3. What is the difference between `<th>` and `<td>`? Why should a table never be used to lay out a whole page?

Your answer:  The '<th>' tag is used for table headings, while the '<td>' tag is used for normal data cells. A table should display information in rows and columns, not arrange an entire webpage, because CSS is better for creating page layouts.

## Q1. In your form, why must a `<label>`'s `for` match its `<input>`'s `id`? What goes wrong if they don't match?

Your answer:
The for attribute of a label must match the id of its input so the browser can connect them. If they do not match, clicking the label will not focus or select the correct input.

## Q2. You used both `<section>` and `<article>`. What is the difference? Why did the hackathon story become an `<article>`?

Your answer:
A <section> groups related content on a webpage, such as a timetable or registration form. An <article> contains independent content that can make sense on its own. The hackathon story is an article because it can be understood separately from the rest of the page.

## Q3. What is the difference between `<th>` and `<td>`? Why should a table never be used to lay out a whole page?

Your answer:
<th> is used for table headings, while <td> is used for table data. A table should not be used to design a whole webpage because tables are meant to organize data in rows and columns, not control the page layout.

## Q1. In your form, why must a `<label>`'s `for` match its `<input>`'s `id`? What goes wrong if they don't match?

Your answer:The for and id connect the label to the correct input field.
If they do not match, clicking the label may not select the correct input field.

## Q2. You used both `<section>` and `<article>`. What is the difference? Why did the hackathon story become an `<article>`?

Your answer:A <section> groups related content, such as a timetable or registration form.
An <article> contains independent content, such as a news story. The hackathon story is an article because it can be understood on its own.

## Q3. What is the difference between `<th>` and `<td>`? Why should a table never be used to lay out a whole page?

Your answer:<th> is used for table headings, while <td> is used for table data.
A table should not be used to design a whole webpage because tables are meant to organize data in rows and columns

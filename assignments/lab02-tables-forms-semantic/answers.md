## Q1. In your form, why must a `<label>`'s `for` match its `<input>`'s `id`? What goes wrong if they don't match?

Your answer:
The for attribute of label must match the id of its input so the browser will know that which input the label belongs to. If they do not match, clicking the label may not select the correct input and form become hard to use.

## Q2. You used both `<section>` and `<article>`. What is the difference? Why did the hackathon story become an `<article>`?

Your answer:
A <section> is used to group content about the same topic while <article> is used for a complete piece of content that can stand on its own. The hackathon story was made in <article> because it tells a whole story that people can read separately.

## Q3. What is the difference between `<th>` and `<td>`? Why should a table never be used to lay out a whole page?

Your answer:
<th> is used for headings in a table while <td> is used for normal data in table. We should not use table to design a whole webpage because tables are used for showing data and using them for page layout makes the website difficult to use and manage.

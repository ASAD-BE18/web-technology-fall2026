## Q1. In your form, why must a `<label>`'s `for` match its `<input>`'s `id`? What goes wrong if they don't match?

Your answer:The for attribute of a label must match the id of its input so that the label is connected to the correct input field. If they do not match, clicking the label may not select the input, and screen readers may not identify the connection correctly.

## Q2. You used both `<section>` and `<article>`. What is the difference? Why did the hackathon story become an `<article>`?

Your answer:A <section> groups related content together, such as a timetable or a join form. An <article> contains independent content, so I used it for the hackathon story because it can be understood as a separate story.

## Q3. What is the difference between `<th>` and `<td>`? Why should a table never be used to lay out a whole page?

Your answer:The <th> element is used for table headings, while <td> is used for ordinary data in the table. A table should not be used to design a whole page because tables are meant to organize data, and using them for page layout makes the structure less accessible and harder to maintain.

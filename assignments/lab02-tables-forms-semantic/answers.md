## Q1. In your form, why must a `<label>`'s `for` match its `<input>`'s `id`? What goes wrong if they don't match?

A <label> is connected to an <input> through its for and id attributes. When both match, clicking the label selects the correct input field. If they do not match, the label may not work properly.

## Q2. You used both `<section>` and `<article>`. What is the difference? Why did the hackathon story become an `<article>`?

A <section> groups related content together, such as a timetable or a join form. An <article> contains a separate story or post that makes sense on its own.

## Q3. What is the difference between `<th>` and `<td>`? Why should a table never be used to lay out a whole page?

The <th> tag is used for table headings, such as Day, Time, and Activity. The <td> tag is used for normal table data, such as Tuesday or 9:00 AM. Tables should be used to show data, not to design the whole webpage, because CSS is better for page layout.
